# Methods

Use for Methods drafting, completeness review, diagnosis, or substantive
restructuring. For a local language edit, preserve a sound existing dependency
structure.

## Scientific function

Methods should make the study inspectable: what entered the study, how the
design created a valid comparison or observation, how measurements were
produced, how raw inputs became reported quantities, and how consequential
uncertainty or validity threats were handled.

Exact replication is not always possible. The reader must still be able to
evaluate validity, compare the work with other studies, and understand the
conditions under which the evidence was produced.

## Recover the method dependency

Use only the roles relevant to the study:

- identity and provenance of samples, sites, participants, datasets, standards,
  or other inputs;
- selection, assignment, controls, comparisons, and study design;
- preparation, intervention, or experimental conditions;
- acquisition, measurement, calibration, and resolution;
- processing and transformations from raw observations to analysable inputs;
- analysis, modelling, simulation, or derived quantities;
- validation, quality control, uncertainty, exclusions, and decision rules;
- linkage from processed outputs to reported Results.

These are diagnostic roles, not required headings.

## Organize by scientific dependency

Choose the organization that makes the procedure easiest to reconstruct. It may
follow a workflow, study design, data lifecycle, experimental system, scientific
question, model, sampling-to-inference chain, multiple evidence streams, shared
protocol with variants, or another dependency present in the work. Do not use
laboratory chronology merely because that is how the work happened.

Consequential methodological information is a hard constraint; prose order is
not. Compress shared procedures, use cross-references where they preserve
recoverability, and keep rationale near a choice when it affects validity or
interpretation.

## Missing information

Never fill a gap with a plausible default. Sample size, replicate count,
instrument setting, exposure condition, software version, statistical test,
random seed, inclusion rule, threshold, calibration, ethics information, or a
design rationale must come from the supplied record.

Ask for or mark missing information when it is necessary for validity,
reproduction, comparison, or accurate interpretation. Do not interrupt merely
to add decorative detail.

## Guardrails

- Do not turn Methods into an equipment catalogue, parameter dump, or generic
  checklist.
- Do not hide a consequential transformation, selection rule, assumption, or
  default.
- Do not present a retrospective analytical choice as prespecified.
- Do not move substantive outcomes into Methods unless a small outcome is
  needed to explain a downstream methodological branch.
- Keep identifiers, units, and method citations consistent with the manuscript.

## Completion check

Every central reported output should be traceable through every applicable
stage, including acquisition, transformation, and analysis where they occur,
with no invented step and no missing condition that would materially change
evaluation of the evidence.
